# Co-op development toolkit for strategic authorities

**From:** Morality Co-operative Ltd (registered society no. 4621, Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014; 2 Queens Road, Haywards Heath, RH16 1EB)
**For:** Sussex & Brighton Strategic Authority (SBSA) and other new strategic authorities
**Status:** Draft offer, 20 October 2026. Nothing here is agreed with SBSA, Co-operatives UK or MHCLG.

---

## The situation

On 25 September 2026 the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (MHCLG) announced a £210m high-streets package. Within it, £20m is split equally between High Street Rental Auctions and a new **£10m regional Co-operative Development Programme**, which will "help mayors and strategic authorities" set up co-operative businesses "owned and run by the staff, customers and communities who use them". The money comes from existing MHCLG high-streets budgets. No rules, allocations or timetable have been published. Co-operatives UK has said it is ready to work with government and strategic authorities on delivery.

SBSA was established on 26 March 2026 (legally the Sussex and Brighton Combined County Authority) covering Brighton & Hove, East Sussex and West Sussex. Its first mayoral election was moved from May 2026 to May 2028. Until then it is run by its constituent councils and has no co-operative development staff or programme.

When allocations arrive, SBSA will need to spend them well, and show that it has, without first building a team.

## What we offer

A small, local delivery layer that sits **under** whatever national arrangement MHCLG sets up, most likely with Co-operatives UK.

| Co-operatives UK (national) | Morality Co-operative (local, Sussex) |
|---|---|
| Model rules and FCA registration route | Help groups choose a model and get to registration |
| Community Shares Standard Mark and practitioner network | Local first contact; referral to licensed practitioners |
| Sector advice, training, national data | Digital and governance layer for each new co-op |
| National programme reporting | A public dashboard of every co-op the programme helps in Sussex |

Concretely, we would provide:

1. **A start-a-co-op guide** for community groups taking on a shop, pub or service ([start-a-coop-guide.md](start-a-coop-guide.md)).
2. **A public dashboard** listing every co-op the programme supports, what it received and whether it is still trading at one, three and five years ([dashboard-spec.md](dashboard-spec.md)). Records are published through the pooter.world Pledge Ledger so that figures cannot be quietly revised.
3. **A digital and governance kit** for each new co-op: member register, online voting and AGM tools, share-offer page, and plain-English board templates.
4. **A 12-month high-street pilot** with one or two Sussex co-ops ([pilot-proposal.md](pilot-proposal.md)).

## What we do not do

- We do not replace Co-operatives UK, Plunkett UK or licensed Community Shares practitioners. We refer to them.
- We do not give regulated financial or legal advice. Groups are pointed to advisers for that.
- We do not hold or manage grant money for co-ops.
- We do not take shares, board seats or fees from the co-ops we help.

## Declared interest

Morality Co-operative operates **pooter.world**, which includes a **Pledge Ledger** that records and tracks the public pledges of strategic authorities, including SBSA. pooter.world may also report on SBSA, its councils and its spending. If we become a delivery partner we would have a financial relationship with SBSA. Our commitments:

- The interest is declared on every relevant page: every pooter.world item and Pledge Ledger record about SBSA or the programme carries a visible note of it.
- People who work on SBSA partnership work do not decide verdicts about SBSA.
- An outside reviewer checks any verdict about SBSA before publication.
- Coverage of SBSA is not shown to SBSA before publication and SBSA has no editorial say.
- Corrections are logged in the public pooter.world corrections register.
- Our own payments from the programme appear on the same public dashboard as everyone else's.

SBSA should weigh this when deciding whether to work with us. We would rather say it now than have it found later.
